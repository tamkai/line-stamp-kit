## Visual Style: Atsunuri (thick painterly) pop sticker — 厚塗り・ポップ・彩度高め

- Chibi proportions: about 2 heads tall (2頭身), big head, small body, oversized expressive face
- Thick-paint ("atsunuri") digital illustration: soft blended shading, visible brush strokes, rich saturated colors, glossy highlights on hair and eyes
- Bold, energetic, kawaii mood. Made to pop on a chat screen at thumbnail size
- Exaggerated, readable emotions: eyes, mouth, eyebrows and hand gestures carry the message

### Sticker outline
- Thick, even WHITE sticker outline (die-cut look) around the whole silhouette — including the text
- Outline width consistent on every stamp

### Cross-stamp consistency (every stamp in this set MUST match)
- EXACT SAME character: same face shape, hairstyle, hair color, eye style, skin tone, outfit and accessories
- EXACT SAME rendering: same brush feel, saturation, shading depth, outline width, head-to-body ratio
- If a reference image or a finished sample stamp is attached, copy its character design and finish exactly; change only pose, expression and text

### TEXT RULE
- Render ONLY the Japanese text given in the spec, verbatim, character by character. No other letters, numbers, logos or watermarks
- Text style: thick hand-lettered marker feel, high contrast, with the same white outline as the character
- Place text near the character (typically above or beside), never covering the face or hands

### Composition
- Square canvas. Character centered, filling roughly 60–75% of the height
- Generous margin of flat key color on all four sides; nothing touches the edge
- No scenery, no ground, no floor, no contact shadow, no frame — only the sticker on the flat key color
