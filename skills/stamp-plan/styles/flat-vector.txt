## Visual Style: Shape-based flat vector — 線なし・図形で作る・モダン

- Proportions: about 2.5 heads tall, built from simple geometric shapes (circles, rounded rectangles, wedges). Hair is one solid silhouette shape
- NO outlines at all. Shapes are separated only by color contrast — like a modern app illustration or an airline safety card
- Fill: flat colors, one flat shadow tone per color placed as a hard-edged shape (no gradients, no soft shading, no highlights)
- Palette: bold and limited — 4–5 saturated but harmonious colors (e.g. mustard, teal, coral, navy) plus skin tone. Deliberately NOT realistic colors: hair may be navy, glasses may be teal
- Face: minimal — two dots or short arcs for eyes, no nose, a simple mouth shape. Glasses as a single flat shape
- Mood: clean, calm, design-conscious. Expression comes from posture and hands more than the face

### Sticker outline
- Even WHITE sticker outline around the whole silhouette, including the text. Medium width, identical on every stamp

### Cross-stamp consistency (every stamp in this set MUST match)
- EXACT SAME character design: face, hairstyle, colors, outfit, accessories
- EXACT SAME line weight, palette, flatness and head-to-body ratio
- If a reference image or a finished sample stamp is attached, copy its design and finish exactly; change only pose, expression and text

### TEXT RULE
- Render ONLY the Japanese text given in the spec, verbatim, character by character. No other letters, numbers, logos or watermarks
- Text style: clean geometric sans-serif (like a modern Japanese gothic typeface), one flat color from the palette, no outline on the letters except the white sticker outline
- Place text near the character, never covering the face or hands

### Composition
- Square canvas. Character centered, filling roughly 60–75% of the height
- Generous margin of flat key color on all four sides; nothing touches the edge
- No scenery, no ground, no floor, no contact shadow, no frame — only the sticker on the flat key color
